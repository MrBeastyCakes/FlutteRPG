## World & Biomes
Elaria consists of three primary regions discovered through exploration:
1. **Whispering Woods**: A lush forest filled with oak and willow trees, wild berries, herbs, and forest beasts like boars and shadow wolves.
2. **Darkstone Mine**: Deep caverns containing copper, tin, and iron ore veins, home to cave spiders and cavern trolls.
3. **Sundered Coast**: A storm-lashed shoreline featuring tide pools, salt flats, and a drowned lighthouse. Includes a real-time weather system (Calm, Sea Fog, Storm Swell) affecting navigation and pearl gathering.
